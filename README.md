# Web3-Digital-Humanities
This is a repo for events, research and open-source tech in the new Web3 Digital Humanities. 

The Web3 Digital Humanities (DH) uses decentralized, open-source tools to fulfill the aims of the original Web1-2 DH, such as the borderless and transparent transmission of information, digitized resources, educational activities, the secure digital preservation of this material, and the constant striving for cleaner, greener digital tech. 

To fulfill the original mission of the Digital Humanities, the Web3 Digital Humanities builds and deploys tools that do not rely on centralized (corporate or state) servers and domain gatekeeping which can, and regularly are, attacked, erased and blocked, as seen recently in late 2026. Instead, Web3 DH strives to build stable global systems that do not require corporations and states, such as mesh networks, distributed ledgers, decentralized AI harnesses in a non-/post-internet. Web3 DH uses immutable decentralized databases secured both algorithmically and by the force of energy production and consumption. An example of such a tool is a simple space heater that uses clean electricity to provide necessary heating, while generating revenue and helping to keep the network free, open and secure. 

The old / existing Web1-2 DH laid the foundations for us, but its tools cannot achieve its aims, in securing pasts and knowledges, in ensuring digital provenance, in democratizing the humanities and education, or at all in its environmentalist intentions: existing Web2 infrastructure prevents it from ever having control of its energy, data or climate impact. Web3 DH incorporates the old but fulfills it in the new, addressing all of the above while also preparing the DH for integrating rapidly progressing AI and preparing for the quantum era.

Web3 DH has a lot of work to do! Come help us out. Build the new era of the Digital Humanities with us.
